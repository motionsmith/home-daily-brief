Copy this to a private workspace if a preferences file is useful. A service list in the automation prompt is enough too. Remove sections you do not need. This file does not configure connectors or grant permissions.

## Day and delivery

- Timezone: your IANA timezone.
- Schedule: your chosen time and days, configured in the host's scheduler.
- Delivery: the current conversation.
- Length: roughly one screen.
- History: none by default; optionally name a private output folder.

Add a row when another service should contribute. The connector must be available in the host. Mark sources that are pending access. If a source is owned by another chat, provide its reference and whether reading or messaging it has been authorized.

## What matters to me

- Protect time for personal priorities and rest.
- Work around known appointments and care commitments.
- Prefer a short plan over a complete inventory of chores.
- Optional: today's capacity, travel constraints, or an outcome I want to make progress on.
